# Solution Overview — BreatheWise AI

## The Urban Air Quality Decision Gap
Urban residents in cities like Delhi, Kolkata, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Asansol encounter rapid diurnal swings in PM2.5 and PM10—especially during winter inversions. Existing AQI apps report an abstract index without accounting for *who* is in the home, *how* they commute, *what* cooking fuel they use, or *what* budget they have.

## How BreatheWise AI Solves It
1. **Environmental Context**: Fetches live Open-Meteo pollutant data (or runs clearly labeled Good/Moderate/Poor/Severe demo scenarios) and translates concentrations into CPCB Indian NAQI categories.
2. **Household & Routine Context**: Captures household composition, lifestyle, commute mode/distance, indoor setup (HEPA purifier, windows, cooking fuel), and budget locally in the browser.
3. **Deterministic + Conversational Intelligence**: Ranks practical, budget-aware actions (*What, Why, When, Benefit, Cost, Impact*) and powers **BreatheWise Intelligence** with structured 7-part guidance—working reliably even with `AI_PROVIDER=none`.
4. **Closed-Loop Sustainability**: Links personal protection choices (switching open-air commutes on severe days to public transit, running kitchen exhaust with covered pots, zero-burning habits) to transparent CO2 avoidance estimates.
